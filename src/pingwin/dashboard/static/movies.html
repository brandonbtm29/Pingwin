                Where reviews are kept

                Everything on these pages (the history, every review, People, watchlists' movie details and IMDb lists) is in state\movielib.json in the shared SyncThing folder, so every PC has it. Pingwin also keeps a dated copy from before each day's first change, for 14 days, in state\backups. To roll back, stop Pingwin and copy one over movielib.json.
